# FitCore Pro — Final Company Demo Script

## 1. Public product
Open the home page. Point out that branch, member, trainer, class and retention figures are calculated from the database. Show the gym slideshow, then open the staff dashboard.

## 2. Executive dashboard
Explain the live chain:
**member activity → attendance/payments → dashboard → analytics/alerts**.

Show active members, monthly revenue, today's check-ins, renewal warning, charts and retention watch.

## 3. Configurable company setup
Open **Settings**. Change or explain:
- company name/contact
- currency symbol
- expiry warning days
- retention lookback
- at-risk visit threshold
- traffic analysis days
- equipment warning days

Then open **Membership Plans** to show that plan price and duration are managed from the website.

## 4. Complete member journey
Open or create a member:
- digital QR card
- membership plan/status
- assigned trainer
- renewal and discount
- freeze/reactivate
- body metrics/progress
- workout plan
- diet plan
- attendance
- payments/receipt

## 5. Front desk
Open **Attendance** and demonstrate QR/manual check-in. Explain automatic membership validation and duplicate-entry protection.

## 6. Fitness delivery
Show:
- Trainers
- Workout Plans
- Diet & Nutrition Plans
- Classes & Booking

Explain that the member portal reads these same records.

## 7. Facility + finance
Show:
- Equipment and maintenance history
- Service cost
- Expenses
- Payments and printable receipt

## 8. Management intelligence
Open **Analytics** and explain revenue/expenses, retention health, membership mix, acquisition and hourly traffic.

Open **Branches** to compare member count, trainer count, visits, monthly revenue, expenses and net position.

## 9. Control and accountability
Open **Notifications** for live calculated alerts, **Global Search**, and **Audit Logs** to show who performed important actions.

## 10. Member experience
Sign out or open another browser tab:
`member@fitcore.com / member123`

Show QR membership card, fitness progress, workout, nutrition plan, class booking, attendance and payment history.

Finish with:
**“FitCore Pro connects customer experience, front-desk operations, fitness delivery, finance, facility management and management intelligence in one database-driven platform.”**
